libevotap.so is evotap, the audio tap of the evo framework: an ALSA plugin
the player's stream passes through byte for byte, measured on the way. The
plugin's level meters read what it measures.

It is a separate work from the plugin, shipped unmodified from its own
release, under the MIT licence; see LICENSE beside this note. Source,
releases and the contract of what it publishes:

    https://github.com/foonerd/evotap

The version shipped is named in build.json at the plugin's root.
