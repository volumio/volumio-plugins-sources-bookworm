audio-keepalive-daemon and libasound_module_pcm_keepalive.so are Audio
Keepalive, the program behind the plugin's Click prevention switch: the
daemon holds the audio output open and passes the music through untouched,
and the players write to it through the ALSA client.

They are a separate work from the plugin, shipped unmodified from its own
release, and are under the GNU General Public License; see LICENSE beside
this note. Source and releases:

    https://github.com/foonerd/alsa-pcm-keepalive

The version shipped is named in build.json at the plugin's root.
